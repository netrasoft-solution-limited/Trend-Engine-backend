Observations

Evidence entered by hand: community posts, social and Shop listings, search figures. Each one keeps its link, its dates and what kind of figure it is, and is labelled when it is an ad, brand content, older material or missing coverage. Attach them to a development on its review page, or to a new-product candidate.

Import a sheet

Download the template, fill one row per observation, save as CSV. A search figure needs its query, provider, geography and period. If any row is wrong nothing is imported, and you are told which rows to fix.
