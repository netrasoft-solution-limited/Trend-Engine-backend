Import a product list or profile

A CSV product list with a name column (optional: kind, terms separated by ;, weight) is added to a copy of the live profile. A JSON profile — the file the Export link on any version downloads — replaces it whole. A full catalogue inventory (columns product and primary_listening_family, like the full-portfolio sheet) replaces the products, assigns each listing to its family, and keeps the live profile's audiences, priorities and any words added by hand. Either way you get a draft to review; nothing goes live until you say so.
